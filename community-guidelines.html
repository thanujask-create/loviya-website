Community Guidelines

Last updated: June 2026

1. Be Real

  • Use your real identity and truthful profile information.
  • Do not use fake, stolen, celebrity, AI-generated, or misleading photos.
  • Do not pretend to be another person.
  • Users may be required to complete verification, selfie checks, live face checks, document verification, or profile photo review when requested.
  • Providing false verification information may result in account restriction, suspension, or removal.

2. Identity Changes

  • Name, calling name, gender, and birthday changes must be truthful.
  • Some identity changes may require admin review before becoming active.
  • During review, LOVIYA may temporarily limit discovery, matching, map visibility, messaging initiation, or profile visibility.
  • False, misleading, or repeated identity change attempts may lead to rejection, re-verification, restriction, or account removal.
  • Gender changes may be subject to platform limits and additional verification checks.

3. Verification Rules

  • LOVIYA may require live face verification, including multiple verification photos.
  • Users must complete verification within the required deadline.
  • If verification is rejected or resubmission is requested, users must respond within the stated deadline.
  • Failure to complete verification or resubmission may lead to account limits, restriction, suspension, or deletion.
  • Verification improves trust but does not guarantee any user’s identity, background, intentions, relationship status, or future behavior.

4. Be Respectful

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, insults, stalking, or hate speech.
  • Respect culture, religion, nationality, gender, language, and personal boundaries.
  • No repeated unwanted messages after someone says no, blocks you, or stops replying.
  • Do not pressure users for romance, sex, photos, money, travel, visas, meetings, or personal information.

5. Keep LOVIYA Safe

  • No scams, fraud, blackmail, money requests, crypto promotion, fake investment offers, or begging.
  • No prostitution, trafficking, illegal services, sexual exploitation, or unsafe activity.
  • No sending or requesting private documents, bank details, passwords, OTP codes, or private addresses.
  • LOVIYA is not an escort service, marriage brokerage, job platform, financial platform, investment platform, or immigration service.

6. Photos & Content

  • No nudity, sexual acts, violent content, hate symbols, drugs, or offensive content.
  • No photos of minors as profile photos.
  • No commercial advertisements, spam, links, or promotional content.
  • Do not upload photos you do not own or do not have permission to use.

7. Chat Rules

  • Be polite and respectful in messages.
  • Do not send explicit images, sexual messages, threats, spam, or private information without consent.
  • LOVIYA may limit the number of unanswered messages that can be sent before a recipient replies.
  • Do not repeatedly start conversations with large numbers of users in a short period of time.
  • Conversation limits, warnings, cooldowns, message restrictions, and risk scoring may be used to reduce spam and improve user safety.
  • Repeated unwanted contact may lead to warnings, restrictions, or account review.

8. Incognito Mode

  • LOVIYA may provide Incognito Mode for eligible female users.
  • Incognito Mode may hide or replace the user’s real name in selected areas of the app.
  • Incognito Mode does not remove verification, reporting, blocking, moderation, or safety checks.
  • Users must not misuse Incognito Mode to mislead, harass, scam, or avoid accountability.

9. Location & Map Safety

  • LOVIYA may provide online user map, nearby user, or location-based discovery features.
  • Users may disable location sharing or hide themselves from map visibility where controls are provided.
  • Do not share exact home addresses, workplace locations, or sensitive private locations in profiles or chats.
  • Location features are intended for discovery and safety, not tracking or harassment.

10. Meeting Safety

  • Meet in public places.
  • Tell a trusted person before meeting someone.
  • Be careful if someone quickly asks to move outside LOVIYA to WhatsApp, Viber, Line, Telegram, or other apps.
  • Never send money or private documents to someone you meet online.

11. Reporting & Blocking

  • Use report and block tools when you see unsafe behavior.
  • False reports or abuse of the reporting system may lead to account action.
  • LOVIYA may review reports, messages, photos, verification status, account status, and safety signals.
  • Users may be asked to complete additional verification or account review when safety concerns are detected.

12. Risk Score & Automated Safety

  • LOVIYA may use automated safety systems, moderation tools, and internal risk scoring to help detect harmful behavior.
  • Safety systems may consider reports, blocks, verification status, messaging patterns, profile activity, moderation history, and other trust signals.
  • Automated systems may apply warnings, message limits, cooldowns, profile visibility limits, verification requests, restrictions, or account review.
  • Risk scoring is used only for safety, moderation, fraud prevention, and community protection.

13. Account Action

LOVIYA may warn, restrict, pause, suspend, place under review, deactivate, or delete accounts that break these guidelines, fail verification requirements, abuse messaging systems, engage in spam-like behavior, misuse identity change features, misuse location features, or create risk for other users.

14. Account Deletion & Recovery

  • Users may request account deletion where available in the app.
  • When deletion is requested, LOVIYA may hide the profile, stop discovery, cancel pending review queues, and limit account activity.
  • Some safety records may be retained when needed for abuse prevention, fraud prevention, dispute handling, safety, or legal compliance.

15. Notifications & Notices

  • LOVIYA may send notifications about verification, matches, messages, reports, restrictions, account actions, safety reminders, and policy updates.
  • Users are responsible for reviewing important LOVIYA notices and responding within required deadlines.
