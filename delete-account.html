Delete Your LOVIYA Account

Google Play Account Deletion Information

Delete Account From Inside The App

LOVIYA allows users to permanently delete their account directly from inside the mobile application.

  • Open LOVIYA.
  • Go to My Profile.
  • Open Account Settings.
  • Select Delete Account.
  • Confirm your deletion request.

What Happens After Requesting Deletion?

  • Your account becomes unavailable to other users.
  • Your profile is removed from discovery and matching features.
  • Your account enters a deletion period before permanent removal.
  • You may contact support if recovery is available during the retention period.

Data Deleted

  • Profile information.
  • Photos uploaded by the user.
  • Dating preferences.
  • Match and interaction records where applicable.

Data That May Be Retained

  • Safety records.
  • Fraud prevention records.
  • Legal compliance records.
  • Moderation and abuse prevention logs where required.
